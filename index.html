Frequently asked questions

What types of packaging services do you offer?

We offer a variety of packaging services including custom crate manufacturing, ocean freight container packing, and expert loadmaster services to ensure safe and efficient transport of your goods.

How long does the packaging process take?

The duration depends on the size and complexity of your shipment. Typically, we can complete standard packaging orders within a few days.

Do you provide international shipping services?

Yes, we specialize in providing international shipping solutions, ensuring that your products reach their destination safely and on time.

What materials do you use for packaging?

We use high-quality, durable materials specifically chosen to protect your items during transit, including wood, cardboard, and other protective materials.

Absolutely! Once your shipment is dispatched, we provide tracking information so you can monitor its progress in real-time.
